## Platform

web

## Users

- **Clients / Members**: Individuals living in or visiting major Indian metros (Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Goa) who seek trusted, platonic company for coffee conversations, workout spotters, concert partners, city walks, or museum visits without awkwardness, dating pressure, or safety anxiety.
- **Companions / Buddies**: Verified local experts, conversationalists, fitness trainers, and city guides offering structured platonic companionship, earning ₹300 to ₹2,000/hr with escrow-backed payment protection.

## Product Purpose

FindCo is India's dedicated social companionship platform. It bridges urban loneliness and social friction by making platonic companionship accessible, safe, and transparent. Success is defined by safe, high-delight companion experiences with verified identity, upfront pricing, and zero safety incidents.

## Positioning

"Friendship-first, always." FindCo is strictly non-romantic and non-escort. Unlike dating apps (Tinder, Bumble) or task gig platforms (Urban Company), FindCo provides:
1. **Government Identity Verification**: Mandatory Aadhaar/biometric audit and Hallmark trust dossiers.
2. **Dual-Handshake PIN Escrow**: Payments are locked in RBI-compliant escrow and only released when both client and companion meet in person and verify mutual PINs.
3. **Platonic Code of Conduct**: Strict automated filtering and immediate ban policies for commercial solicitation or harassment.

## Operating Context

- **Geographies**: Major Indian metropolitan hubs: Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Goa.
- **Activity Verticals**: 14+ curated categories including Specialty Coffee Trails, Gym & Fitness Spotting, Nightout & Drive Buddy, Heritage City Walks, Co-working Sprints, Concerts & Fest Buddies.
- **Device Context**: Responsive Progressive Web App (mobile web touch-first + widescreen desktop dashboard).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Real-time Discovery**: Filter companions by city, hourly rate slider (₹300 - ₹2,000/hr), Hallmark verification status, and activity category.
- **Hallmark Trust Dossier**: Cryptographic audit modal inspecting government ID status, selfie face-match score, and police verification status.
- **Dual Mode**: Instant toggle between Client Mode (booking buddies) and Buddy Mode (accepting requests and managing earnings).
- **In-App Messaging & Booking Escrow**: Private chat channel unlocked upon active membership pass or booking confirmation.
- **Technical Constraints**: Client-side React 19 SPA running on Vite, Supabase authentication & Postgres DB, Motion animation engine, and CSS custom property design system.

## Product Principles

1. **Platonic Integrity Above All**: Uncompromising adherence to strict friendship-first standards; zero tolerance for romance solicitation or commercial escorting.
2. **Cryptographic & Escrow Security**: Both parties protected via government-verified identity and mutual PIN dual-handshake before escrow funds release.
3. **Radical Transparent Pricing**: Hourly rates clearly shown upfront with no hidden platform surge fees.
4. **Low-Friction Discovery**: Instant categorized intent matching (Hick's Law) and seamless communication.
